Transformations
---------------
The source records were filtered to entries with usable definitions, reduced to
the supported part-of-speech groups, normalized into NudeNyang's JSON pack
schema, and compressed. Examples, audio, images, and separately licensed media
are not included. The expanded packs retain their source and licence metadata.
