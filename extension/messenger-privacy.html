Web translation privacy notice

F4 and the switch control automatic translation in all tabs, including new tabs. This includes visible webpage text and the current messenger conversation, using the translator selected in the Windows app.

X · Discord · WhatsApp · Telegram · Messenger · Slack · Microsoft Teams · Google Messages

  • Message bodies and link previews in the open conversation, and visible channel names in the current Discord server, are passed to the Windows app.
  • The translator selected in the app is used. Local models process text on your PC. Selecting an external service (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or DeepL) sends conversations to that service under its policies.
  • Source text and translations are stored in the app's encrypted cache, following its retention period (30 days by default) and history deletion settings. Switching conversations or withdrawing consent does not delete existing cache entries. Private browsing content is not stored.
  • In private browsing, use a local model or DeepL. Local records created by subscription CLIs cannot be controlled.
  • No message composition or sending features are used, and user names and contacts are not translated.
